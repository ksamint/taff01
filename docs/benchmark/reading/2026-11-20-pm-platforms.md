# Reading: project-management platforms beyond Linear

**Origin:** research written by another assistant and supplied by the owner on
2026-11-20. Condensed here; the judgements are the original author's. It was
written from a summary of Taff, without access to this repository. Product
claims come from vendor pages and were not tested by hand. Two claims were
checked for this record (see the end).

**Headline:** Asana is the first all-round recommendation beyond Linear.
Jira, monday.com, ClickUp, Wrike and Smartsheet belong on the main shortlist.
For Taff, add Basecamp, Planview, Oracle Primavera and Teamwork.com or
Kantata, because they carry management ideas an issue-tracker comparison
misses.

## 1. Platforms by what they do best

### A. Work across an organization

| Platform | Shortlist it for | Worth studying | Consider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Asana](https://asana.com/) | Cross-functional work; best all-round start | Connects tasks, projects, portfolios and goals. One task can sit in several projects as one shared record. | Agree how goals, portfolios and project ownership are used. |
| [monday.com](https://monday.com/) | Configurable business workflows | Enterprise tier: cross-project dependencies, resource directory, capacity planning, portfolio dashboards, managed templates that propagate changes. | Fix common fields and templates early. |
| [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/) | One workspace for tasks, documents, chat and AI | Tasks, Docs, Chat, dashboards, whiteboards, time tracking, automation; a task can sit in several lists. | Keep the first configuration small. |
| [Wrike](https://www.wrike.com/) | Complex delivery with reviews and approvals | Resource planning, request intake, workflows, visual proofing, file versions, internal and external approvals. | The reference when review and revision are a large part of the work. |
| [Smartsheet](https://www.smartsheet.com/) | Repeatable programmes and a project office | Control Center: project blueprints, automated project creation, controlled change, portfolio reporting. | Control Center is an add-on. |

### B. Software delivery

| Platform | Shortlist it for | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) | Complex engineering workflows across teams | A transition can have permissions, conditions and validators. Plans combine teams, show dependencies and allow scenarios before committing. |
| [Shortcut](https://www.shortcut.com/) | A focused alternative to Linear | Stories, epics, iterations, objectives and key results, with workflow limits and delivery reporting. |
| [YouTrack](https://www.jetbrains.com/youtrack/) | Adaptable engineering management | Issues, configurable boards, scripted workflows, Gantt, time tracking, knowledge base. |

**Named as the most important reference for Taff's execution rules:** Jira. A
workflow can define who may change a state, what must be true first, and
what happens afterwards. That matters most when people and agents share work.

### C. Portfolios and formal project control

| Platform | Shortlist it for | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Planview Portfolios](https://www.planview.com/products-solutions/products/planview-portfolios/) | Investment and portfolio decisions | Funding, demand evaluation, capacity planning, scenarios, programme dependencies, forecast against actual cost. |
| [ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management](https://www.servicenow.com/products/strategic-portfolio-management.html) | Demand, governance and transformation portfolios | Intake, capacity, financial review, approvals, delivery and resources in one chain. |
| [Oracle Primavera](https://www.oracle.com/construction-engineering/primavera-cloud-project-management/) | Engineering and schedule-heavy projects | Critical-path scheduling, resources, risk and capital planning. The reference for what makes a schedule credible. |
| [Microsoft Planner premium, Project desktop](https://www.microsoft.com/en/microsoft-365/planner/microsoft-planner-business-plans-and-pricing) | Microsoft-centred organizations | Dependencies and critical path. The research states Project Online retired on 2026-09-30 and that Planner, Project desktop and Project Server are unaffected. |

### D. Consulting, agencies and client delivery

| Platform | Shortlist it for | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Teamwork.com](https://www.teamwork.com/) | Client projects and delivery economics | Client work, resource planning, time tracking, budgets, cost and profitability side by side. |
| [Kantata](https://www.kantata.com/psa) | Larger professional-services operations | Delivery joined to staffing, forecasting, finance, invoicing and utilization. |

The research gives this category substantial weight for a consulting-oriented
Taff. The question a project dashboard should answer: *are we delivering the
agreed result with the right people, within budget, at an acceptable margin?*

## 2. Other benchmarks worth including

- **[Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/) and Shape Up.** Hill charts show
  whether a scope is still being figured out or is being executed. Appetite
  sets how much time a problem deserves. Shape Up is the method; Basecamp is
  the product. The question it adds: *do we understand how to finish this?*
- **[Notion](https://www.notion.com/product/projects):** knowledge and
  context beside execution. **[Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/platform/connected-data):**
  different views over shared business objects.
  **[Productboard](https://www.productboard.com/platform/):** evidence
  feeding the decision about what to do.
- **[OpenProject](https://www.openproject.org/community-edition/)** and
  **[Plane](https://github.com/makeplane/plane):** open source, so the
  implementation can be read. OpenProject covers work packages, phases,
  milestones, dependencies and multi-project timelines.

## 3. Design questions and their benchmark

| Design question | Benchmark | Carry into Taff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How does work connect to purpose? | Asana | Clear relations between goals, projects, tasks and portfolios. |
| What rules govern execution? | Jira | Explicit states, permitted transitions, required evidence, approval conditions. |
| How to manage uncertainty and limit investment? | Basecamp, Shape Up | Uncertainty per scope, appetite, decisions to cut scope. |
| Which work gets scarce resources? | Planview | Demand evaluation, capacity limits, funding, scenarios. |
| What makes the schedule credible? | Primavera | Dependency logic, resource limits, schedule control. |
| Is client work commercially successful? | Teamwork.com, Kantata | Commitments, staffing, actual cost, utilization, profitability. |
| How to repeat a successful delivery? | Smartsheet, Wrike | Templates, intake, reviews, approvals, controlled process change. |
| How should people and agents share work? | Asana, ClickUp | Shared context, agent permissions, review checkpoints, visible execution. |

## 4. Two distinctions the research asks Taff to keep

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | A desired result, preferably with a measure and a target. |
| Initiative | A coordinated effort that advances one or more goals. |
| Portfolio | A collection of investments or work managed together. |
| Project | A bounded delivery effort. |

A **stage** (here: phase) is a span of work; a **milestone** is a checkpoint.
Linear lets teams group issues into project milestones, but a system for
broader project management should keep the two apart.

## 5. Positioning

The research says "people and AI agents manage projects together" is already
an established direction, citing Asana AI Teammates and ClickUp Super Agents.
Its proposed distinction for Taff:

> Manage people and agents against explicit commitments, with traceable
> execution, controlled cost, and accepted deliverables.

Tested by five questions: acceptance (what evidence shows the result),
accountability (who executed, verified, accepted), economics (what the
accepted result cost, with failed attempts and rework), interoperability
(agents from different providers under the same rules), control (pause for
information, approval or budget without losing history).

**Its recommended research order:** Asana, Jira, Basecamp; then Teamwork.com
or Kantata, Planview, Primavera.
